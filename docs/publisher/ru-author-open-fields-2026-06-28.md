## Front matter

### Об авторе

Fill in the Google Doc and keep a synchronized repository note:

- public name or author name;
- current role or independent positioning;
- verified professional experience;
- public projects;
- public links: GitHub, website, blog, social profile or company page;
- public companion URL, release version and errata route;
- publisher-facing short bio;
- publisher-facing long bio;
- preferred Russian wording for the author line.

Do not let the current placeholder ship as final copy.

### Author platform note

Fill:

- why the author is credible for this topic;
- which parts of the book come from practical work;
- which examples are original, anonymized or composite;
- which public project or companion material supports the book.

### Acknowledgements

Decide:

- whether acknowledgements are included;
- names or groups to thank;
- whether any permission is needed before publication.

## Book metadata

Fill or approve:

- final Russian title;
- subtitle;
- short cover copy;
- long catalog/website description;
- target reader wording;
- keywords;
- author contact for publisher metadata.

## Companion

Fill:

- public companion URL;
- repository URL if public;
- release version for the book edition;
- contribution/errata process;
- whether issues/discussions are enabled;
- license or usage terms for templates.

## Legal, compliance and AI-use wording

Author/publisher must approve:

- disclaimer that templates are not legal or compliance advice;
- AI tooling disclosure for manuscript and companion preparation;
- privacy/security note for examples;
- statement about anonymized or composite cases;
- jurisdiction-specific limitations if the publisher requires them.

## Real cases

Decide:

- which real implementation stories can be included;
- which stories must remain composite;
- which details must be anonymized;
- whether any employer/client/project approval is required.

## Final author checklist

- [ ] Fill `Об авторе` short and long versions.
