Goal: make author-owned and front-matter fields explicit before the final
editorial proof cycle without inventing factual author data.

## Result

The full Google Doc manuscript was updated in the `Об авторе` block. The old
short `Факты для заполнения перед публикацией` list was replaced with a fuller
author-owned field list:

- role or current positioning;
- verified experience;
- public projects;
- public links;
- companion URL, book release version and errata route;
- publisher-facing author wording;
- acknowledgements decision;
- legal disclaimer and AI tooling disclosure;
- final title/subtitle/cover-copy/imprint metadata.

This pass deliberately did not fill author facts, external links, biography,
acknowledgements or legal wording. Those remain author/publisher-owned.
