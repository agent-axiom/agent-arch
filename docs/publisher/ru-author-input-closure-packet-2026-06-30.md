Date: 2026-06-30.

Status: ready for author completion. These fields are intentionally not filled
by Codex.

## Required from the author

Fill these before final publisher submission:

1. Public byline exactly as it should appear in the book.
2. Short author bio: 2-4 sentences for front matter and cover note.
3. Long author bio: publisher metadata/promotional version.
4. Current role/title wording.
5. Verifiable experience claims that may be printed.
6. Public projects that are safe to mention.
7. Public links: GitHub, site, blog, profile or explicit omission.
8. Companion URL, version route, changelog route and errata route.
9. Final title/subtitle/cover-copy approval.
10. Legal/compliance disclaimer.
11. AI-use disclosure if required by the publisher.
12. Acknowledgements or explicit omission.
13. Real/composite/anonymized case policy.
14. Publisher metadata fields.

fields, but it must not be labeled final publisher submission until the author
fills, omits or delegates them to the publisher questionnaire.
