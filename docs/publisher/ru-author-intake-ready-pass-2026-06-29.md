# Author intake ready pass

Date: 2026-06-29.

Status: implemented as a safe author-owned intake layer. No author facts were
invented or inferred.

## What is ready

The manuscript package now has a stable place for author-owned facts. The
working rule is simple: final publisher export may proceed only after these
fields are filled by the author, explicitly omitted, or moved to a separate
publisher questionnaire.

Required author inputs:

- public author byline;
- short author bio for front matter and cover note;
- longer author bio for publisher metadata and promotion;
- current role/title wording;
- verified experience claims;
- public projects that are safe to mention;
- public links;
- companion URL and release/version route;
- title, subtitle and cover-copy approval;
- legal/compliance disclaimer;
- AI-use disclosure, if the publisher requires it;
- acknowledgements;
- real/composite/anonymized case policy;
- publisher metadata fields.

## Current decision

The manuscript may keep a placeholder author block for editorial routing, but
the final book text must not present unverified biography, credentials, client
claims, work history or public links. The author block is therefore treated as
ready for intake, not as closed content.

## Result

Implemented:

- author-owned fields are listed in
  `docs/publisher/ru-author-owned-final-inputs-2026-06-29.md`;
- final-export gating is explicit;
- no author facts were generated by Codex;
- the field list can be reused in the editor/publisher packet.

Still open:

- author must fill or explicitly waive the fields above;
- final cover note must use the author-approved short byline;
- final DOCX export should wait until the author decision is recorded.
