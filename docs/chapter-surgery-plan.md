# Chapter Surgery Plan

## Goal

Move the manuscript from a strong handbook/reference project toward a stronger book by improving argument shape, reader journey, case continuity, role clarity between adjacent chapters, and explicit promise-shape across parts and entry surfaces.

A key constraint now is that the book must own argument and reader transformation, while support layers own schemas, runnable anchors, templates, and contract detail. The surgery plan should protect that boundary, not blur it.

## Surgery priorities

### Priority 1. Opening and framing
Files likely involved:
- docs/index*.md
- docs/start-here*.md
- docs/book/part-i/index*.md
- docs/book/part-i/chapter-1*
- docs/book/part-i/chapter-2*

Actions:
- sharpen the promise of the book as a transformation in thinking
- reduce handbook/site energy in entry surfaces
- make Chapter 1 more polemical and memorable
- make Chapter 2 more obviously the first proof of the thesis, not just an architecture walkthrough

### Priority 2. Reader-journey transitions
Files likely involved:
- part indexes across I, V, VII, VIII
- selected chapter intros and endings

Actions:
- give each part one dominant question
- ensure each part closes by creating the need for the next one
- reduce the feeling of topic accumulation
- rewrite indexes and entry surfaces until they sound like outcome promises, not topic lists

### Priority 4. Evidence reinforcement

### Priority 5. Book/reference separation
Files likely involved:

- schema pages
- selected late chapters

Actions:

- push exhaustive contract detail into appendix/reference where it interrupts reading flow
- ensure snippets prove points rather than decorate claims
