# Chapter Surgery Plan

## Goal

Move the manuscript from a strong handbook/reference project toward a stronger book by improving argument shape, reader journey, case continuity, and role clarity between adjacent chapters.

## Surgery priorities

### Priority 1. Opening and framing
Files likely involved:
- docs/index*.md
- docs/start-here*.md
- docs/book/part-i/index*.md
- docs/book/part-i/chapter-1*
- docs/book/part-i/chapter-2*

Actions:
- sharpen the promise of the book as a transformation in thinking
- reduce handbook/site energy in entry surfaces
- make Chapter 1 more polemical and memorable
- make Chapter 2 more obviously the first proof of the thesis, not just an architecture walkthrough

### Priority 2. Reader-journey transitions
Files likely involved:
- part indexes across I, V, VII, VIII
- selected chapter intros and endings

Actions:
- give each part one dominant question
- ensure each part closes by creating the need for the next one
- reduce the feeling of topic accumulation

### Priority 3. Late-book role separation
Files likely involved:
- chapter-11*
- chapter-12*
- chapter-13*
- chapter-21*
- chapter-22*
- chapter-25*
- chapter-26*
- chapter-27*
- part-v/index*
- part-viii/index*

Actions:
- cut overlap
- reinforce unique jobs-to-be-done
- preserve the new role grammar:
  - tracing = raw evidence capture
  - SLO = health and risk budgets
  - evals = judgment
  - assurance = response
  - provenance/artifacts = evidence backbone
  - observability = evidence substrate
  - registry = estate accountability
- make transitions explicit instead of allowing adjacent chapters to collapse into one broad governance blob

### Priority 4. Evidence reinforcement
Files likely involved:
- appendix sources
- selected chapter footnotes and section support
- possibly why-this-book / plan pages

Actions:
- increase evidence diversity
- reduce overreliance on vendor-doc authority alone
- add more postmortem/research/governance classes where argument stakes are highest

### Priority 5. Book/reference separation
Files likely involved:
- appendix reference-package*
- schema pages
- selected late chapters

Actions:
- keep book chapters focused on causal explanation and trade-offs
- push exhaustive contract detail into appendix/reference where it interrupts reading flow
- ensure snippets prove points rather than decorate claims

## Current editorial geometry checkpoint

Already established in manuscript and should now be treated as a constraint, not an experiment:

- Part V = capture, health, judgment
- Part VIII = response, evidence backbone, judgment under pressure, evidence substrate, accountability

If a future rewrite weakens those distinctions, it should be treated as regression, not stylistic drift.
