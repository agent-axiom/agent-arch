## Release versioning rule

- `v1.0-book`: materials matching the first published book edition.
- `v1.0.x`: typo fixes and small clarifications.
- `v1.1`: new templates, new cases or materially changed guidance.
- `main` or `latest`: current working state, may be ahead of the printed book.
